04 / Interpretation

With more states in the same voltage span, adjacent thresholds get closer. At a fixed noise level the decoder becomes more error-prone. This is why real multilevel storage requires calibration, read-margin design, and error correction.

No actual hardware voltage, power consumption or error-correction rates have been measured here. The model is for conceptual comparison only.
