Camp Critters

A whole alternate-timeline country where the forests never stopped growing. Start at your campsite in Clover Bay on the Pacific, then travel east through Portland, the Gorge, Boise, Salt Lake City, the Rockies and Denver to Garden City, Kansas. Own houses, knock on any door, run shops and jobs, catch bugs and fish for the museum, hatch creatures in the Sky Sanctuary, and camp with friends in shared worlds with voice chat.
